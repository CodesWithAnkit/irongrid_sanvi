# Product Overview

## IronGrid - Sanvi Machinery B2B Quotation & CRM Platform

IronGrid is a comprehensive B2B quotation and CRM platform designed specifically for Sanvi Machinery's industrial equipment business. The platform streamlines the entire sales process from lead generation to order fulfillment.

### Core Business Domain
- **Industry**: Industrial machinery and equipment
- **Market**: B2B customers in India
- **Primary Use Case**: Quotation generation, customer relationship management, and order processing

### Key Features
- **Live Quotation Builder**: Real-time quotation creation with Indian market specifics (INR formatting, GST calculations: 9% SGST + 9% CGST)
- **Customer Management**: Comprehensive B2B customer profiles with interaction history and credit management
- **Order Processing**: Complete workflow from quotation approval to order fulfillment
- **Analytics & Reporting**: Business intelligence with conversion tracking and performance metrics
- **Email Automation**: Automated quotation delivery, follow-ups, and customer communication

### Business Rules
- All monetary values must be displayed in INR format
- GST calculations: 9% SGST + 9% CGST (total 18%)
- Amount in words must be displayed on quotations (Indian format)
- Print-ready layouts must align with Sanvi Machinery branding
- High-value quotations require approval workflow
- Customer credit limits must be enforced

### Target Users
- Sales representatives
- Sales managers
- Operations managers
- Business owners
- Customer service teams
